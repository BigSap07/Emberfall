that needs someone who draws or who knows the drawings. Every question about ability wording and
game mechanics went to a separate list for Matthias, so none of it is here.

**How it is ordered.** By how much each item unblocks, most first. An item marked **BLOCKING** stops
something in the game from working or from being built at all until it arrives. Everything else
makes the game look better without holding anything up.

## 1. Celen's battle sheets (BLOCKING)

**The problem.** Celen, the 4-star Tide Warden, and Nereia, the 5-star Tide Mender, were delivered
with the same battle body. Four of the seven sheets are identical file for file and the other three
differ by a few hundred pixels. The profile icons settled which body is whose: Celen's icon is an
avian character in blue and white with a war fan, and Nereia's is a hooded figure with a lantern
staff wreathed in blue flame. **The body both of them fight in is Nereia's.** Nothing avian appears
in either delivery.

**Why it blocks.** A player who owns both sees the same character twice in one battle, and Celen
is the only 4-star with drawn battle art, so she is the one 4-star a player can judge the art by.
Nereia's sheets are correct and need nothing.

**What is needed.** A full set for the character on Celen's icon: idle, basic attack, second basic
attack, skill, hit reaction, death and the stunned loop, drawn the same way as the other 5-star
sets.

## 2. Rheia's skill sheet (BLOCKING)

**The problem.** Rheia's delivery contained a second idle sheet where the skill sheet should have
been. When she casts her active skill, the game has nothing to play, so she keeps standing idle
while the skill's effects land around her.

**What is needed.** One skill sheet for Rheia. The extra idle sheet can be dropped when it arrives.

## 3. Strike frames on five heroes

Every attack sheet has one frame where the blow lands. The game hangs the damage number, the hit
flash and the target's flinch on that frame, so a wrong choice shows as a number appearing while
the hero is still winding up, or after they have already recovered. The frame was picked by a
measurement that works for side swings and fails on overhead swings and thin arrows, and sixteen
clips were corrected by eye after that. **Five heroes still read wrong in battle:**

| hero | what is known |
|---|---|
| **Mireth** | Both basic attacks were corrected once already, because her hat was fooling the measurement. Her skill was left where the measurement put it, as a near tie. |
| **Hale** | All three of his attack clips were set by hand, because his arrow is too thin to measure. They were set by an engineer, not by you. |
| **Rheia** | None of her clips has been reviewed by hand. Her skill has no sheet yet (item 2), so name its strike frame when it is drawn. |
| **Oris** | None of Oris's clips has been reviewed by hand. |
| **Arden** | None of Arden's clips has been reviewed by hand. |

Nobody wrote down which clip on each hero looks wrong, so please look at every attack clip on all
five.

**What is needed.** For each attack clip on these five, the number of the frame where the weapon
reaches the target or the released effect first appears. That is the rule the other corrections
followed. The team can send a contact sheet for each hero, with every frame of each attack laid
out in a row and the current choice boxed, so this can be done without opening the game.

**For every future delivery**, please name the strike frame for each attack sheet when you hand it
over. That removes the guess completely.

## 5. New enemies, walls first (BLOCKING for the guild dungeon)

**The problem.** The enemy roster is thin. The guild dungeon builds each floor around a kind of
behaviour, and one of those kinds is a **wall**: an enemy that holds the front line with a taunt or
very heavy armour while others deal damage behind it. **Only one enemy in the game works as a
wall, the Stone Warden**, so every wall floor in every guild's dungeon leads with the same Stone
Warden, always of the Verdant aspect.

**What is needed from you** is who the new enemies are and what they look like. The team can give
them stats and abilities. A proposed brief:

- **Two or three new walls, each of a different aspect from Verdant**: for example an Ember, a Tide
  and a Lumen front-liner. One elite-sized, the others smaller.
- After that, anything that fills the thinnest aspects: Gale has only the Grave Moth and Lumen only
  the Fractured Chorister.

A new enemy can enter the game on the placeholder look before its art exists, so these can be
designed on paper first and drawn later. The design is the part nobody else can do.

## 7. Battle sheets for the 63 heroes on the mannequin

**The state.** 31 of the 94 heroes have battle art: all thirty 5-stars and Celen (whose art is
item 1). The other 63 fight as a tinted placeholder mannequin with their name under its feet. That
is every 1-star (8), every 2-star (12), every 3-star (18) and 25 of the 26 4-stars. All 94 profile
icons are done.

**Why it matters more than it looks.** Summons land on these tiers 97 times in 100: one pull in five
is a 1-star, three in ten a 2-star, three in ten a 3-star, about one in six a 4-star, and three in a
hundred a 5-star. So nearly every hero a player pulls fights as a mannequin.

**Smaller sets for the low tiers.** A 1-star or 2-star hero has no active skill, so a skill sheet
would never play, and the stunned loop plays only rarely (item 9). **For the 20 one- and
two-star heroes, four sheets are enough: idle, basic attack, hit reaction and death.** A second
basic attack sheet is a bonus, not a requirement. 3-stars and 4-stars need the skill sheet too.

**Proposed order:**

1. **The 18 three-stars.** Three pulls in ten, and the highest tier a new player gets often enough to
   field.
2. **The 25 four-stars.** Rarer, but the heroes a player keeps in the team longest.
3. **The 12 two-stars**, at four sheets each.
4. **The 8 one-stars**, at four sheets each.

## 8. The summon screen's replaceable pieces

Every piece of the summon animation is currently built from generic glows and shapes. Each one can
be swapped for a painted image without any other change. Ordered by how much each would add:

1. **The star.** The reveal shows the new hero's star rating, and at that size the current star reads
   as a pentagon. A painted star would make a 5-star reveal land much harder. Smallest job, biggest
   difference.
2. **The flash.** The moment before the reveal is a flat white rectangle, which reads as a cut rather
   than light. A radial white burst would read as light.
3. **The burst, rings and rays.** The glow behind the reveal is three stacked soft glows, the rings
   are plain circles, the light rays are stretched glows and the sparks are small glows. A painted
   burst, ring and spark set **for each of the five rarity colours** (gold, purple, blue, green,
   grey) would carry far more character.
4. **A summon-size rarity frame.** The revealed hero sits in the same portrait frame every other
   screen uses, just larger. A frame or painted card made for the reveal would make it feel like an
   event.
5. **Optional: a reveal background** in each rarity colour. It would help a 3-star reveal most,
   which has the least going on.

## 9. Missing second-basic and stunned sheets on drawn heroes

Every sheet you delivered now plays in battle. Heroes alternate their two basic attack sheets, and
the stunned sheet replaces the idle while a hero is stunned. Some drawn heroes are short:

- **Second basic attack** missing for **Aeron, Dahlia, Merek, Oris, Talren, Vaelis and Zella.** Until
  it arrives, every basic plays the first one, which is fine but repetitive.
- **Stunned loop** missing for **Aeron, Dahlia, Merek, Mireth, Oris and Zella.** Only one ability in
  the game can stun, Corin's Weight of Time, and until recently it never landed at all. **It lands
  now, but rarely**: in about 3 of every 100 battles Corin fights. A hero's stunned sheet plays when
  Corin stuns that hero from the other side, which happens in the Proving and in friend duels, so
  the stunned sheets already delivered are now seen in play. The missing six are still low on this
  list; they are no longer art that nobody will see.

## 10. Notes for future paintings

- **Hub: keep the outer edges clear.** The home screen's current painting puts the Proving's
  gatehouse hard against the right edge, where the screen's column of four round buttons sits on top
  of painted buildings. For anything painted from now on, **leave the outer 7% of both the left and
  right edges as landscape rather than buildings.** The left edge of the current painting already
  does this. Nothing needs repainting now unless you want to fix the right edge.
- **Optional: rarity-coloured badge frames.** Every profile icon carries the same gold frame, so the
  game shows rarity with a coloured plate behind the icon instead. Five recoloured frames (gold,
  purple, blue, green, grey) would drop straight in. Only worth doing if the frame is already a
  separate layer in your source files; not worth redrawing any icon for.
