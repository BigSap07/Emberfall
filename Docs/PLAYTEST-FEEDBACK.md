# Emberfall - playtest feedback

Short answers are fine. "I do not know" and "nothing" are real answers. Be blunt: a bored or
confused tester tells us more than a polite one.

## After your first session

1. How long did you play, roughly, and did you stop because you wanted to or because you had to?
2. In your own words, what is the game about? What were you trying to do?
3. Did the first ten minutes make sense? Was there a moment you did not know what to press?
4. Where did you get stuck, and what did you do to get unstuck?
5. Where were you bored? Name the moment, even if it was short.
6. Could you tell what was happening in a battle? Who was hitting whom, who was winning, who
   was about to act?
7. Did the numbers and bars on screen (health, the turn order, the status icons) help, or get in
   the way?
8. Did the STATS screen after a wave tell you anything useful?
9. Did you see a locked button with an UNLOCKS AT tag? Did you understand what it meant?
10. Did you want to keep going when you stopped? What would make you open it again tomorrow?

## After day two

Play again a day later, then answer:

1. Did you come back by choice? What brought you back, or what nearly did not?
2. What did you do first, and was it clear what to do with your time?
3. What is the one thing you would change before showing this to a friend?

## If something breaks

Send us two things.

- **The build line.** It is in small letters in the bottom-left corner of the main screen and
  looks like `BUILD 0.3.0 (` and seven letters and numbers and `)`. The file `BUILD.txt` in the
  game's folder has it too.
- **Your save folder, zipped.** Open this in File Explorer by pasting it into the address bar:

  ```
  %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Emberfall Studio\Emberfall\
  ```

  Close the game, go up one level, right-click the `Emberfall` folder, choose **Send to**, then
  **Compressed (zipped) folder**, and send the zip. It holds your save and the game log. It holds
  no account or password, because this build has none.

Then tell us what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
