# Play stats and your privacy

This page is for playtesters. It is short on purpose.

## The builds you may have

The playtest zips you were sent are offline. They never ask the question below and send nothing at all. This page applies to builds that do ask.

## What the game asks

Early on, a build that supports it asks: "Share anonymous play stats to help us improve the game? No account, no name, no personal data."

The answer is **No** unless you click or tap **Yes**. You can change your mind later.

## If you say Yes, we get play counts

- When you start and stop playing, and for how long.
- Which tutorial step you reach.
- Which stage, wave and battle you reach, whether you win or lose, and how strong your team was.
- The game version, the version of your save format, and whether you are on Windows or Android.
- How many level ups and summons you do.
- Which screens you open.

We use this to see where players get stuck.

## What is never sent

- Your name.
- An account of any kind.
- Anything you type.
- A device id.
- Your save.

Your IP address is not put in our table. The hosting service that receives the counts may log connection addresses, the way any website does.

Each player gets a random id made at the moment you say Yes. It is not linked to who you are.

## Turning it off

Open Settings and switch play stats off. This deletes your random id and anything not yet sent. Nothing is sent after that. We cannot delete your earlier counts one by one, because we cannot tell which are yours. They are all deleted when the playtest ends, and no later than 90 days. Turning stats on again makes a new random id.

## How long we keep it

Until the playtest ends, and at most 90 days.

## Who to ask

The person who sent you the game.
