# R28 screens, builder b: the guild dungeon ladder and the rewarded ads, every state the spec lists, at both shapes.
#
#   powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File Tools/capture-batch.ps1 `
#       -TargetsFile Tools/capture-r28b-targets.txt -LogDir D:\Emberfall\handoff\<RUN>\logs -Tag r28b
#
# Screen:GuildLadder=<state> puts an in-memory guild in front of the real controller (GuildDungeonBackends.Override).
#   offline-nocloud, offline-signedout (TRY AGAIN), unavailable (FIND A GUILD), loading, error (TRY AGAIN) are the status
#   states; g4-open (a guild of 4 on cycle open), g30-cap (a guild of 30 at the cycle cap), g12-grew (a guild of 12 whose
#   cycle opened at 9), changed-guild (15 attempts spent in another guild) and after-floor8 (floors 1 to 8 cleared, the
#   muted "Reward not decided yet" on every row) are the five states GUILD-DUNGEON-SIZE.md asks for; banner-refresh,
#   banner-offline, banner-refused and banner-cycleover are a ladder under each of its four banners.
#
# Screen:AdClaim=<state>, Screen:AdProving=<state>, Screen:AdStage=<state> are the rewarded ad block in its three places
#   (A1 the idle claim popup after CLAIM, A2 the Proving beside the Brands, A3 the stage cleared view). The states are
#   hidden (AdsSwitch off: nothing is drawn), ready, playing, granted, notfinished, failed, refused. AdsSwitch is flipped
#   ON for the capture only (Stop puts it back) and the simulated ad backend stands in for the network. They rewrite the
#   scratch profile, so they only run on the scratch identity (capture-batch.ps1 always is). AdStage hands the stage cleared
#   view a repeat clear directly (the game sweeps repeats and never draws them on this view). Every ad target first
#   empties the scratch profile's ads block, so one granted target does not cap the ones after it. Batches of 10 at most:
#   Tools/capture-r28b-retry-1.txt to -6.txt.
#
# The size is the Game view's: 1920x1080 is 16:9, 2400x1080 is 20:9.
